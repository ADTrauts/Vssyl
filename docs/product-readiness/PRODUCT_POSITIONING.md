## What Vssyl is

**Vssyl is an AI-native modular workspace platform** that unifies personal and business work under one account:

- **Platform layer** — authentication, billing, search, AI context, notifications, policy engine
- **Workspace archetypes** — Personal Dashboard and Business Workspace (not interchangeable)
- **Dashboard** — tabbed layout surface where widgets and module hubs live
- **Applications** — installed via Marketplace; built-in modules (Drive, Chat, Calendar, AI) ship with platform
- **Content** — files, messages, events, and records owned by modules within scoped workspaces

**In customer language:**

> One workspace for your files, conversations, calendar, and AI — extend with apps from the marketplace when you need more.

---

## What Vssyl is not

| Vssyl is not | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| A single-purpose app (chat-only, files-only) | It is a **workspace shell** hosting modules |
| Replacing every SaaS tool day one | Modules extend capability; bootstrap provides core trio |
| An autonomous AI agent that acts without user intent | AI augments within authorized module context |
| A public app store with instant partner self-serve scale | Certification is operator-mediated today |
| An enterprise-ready SOC2/HIPAA-certified product **yet** | Claims on landing exceed public evidence |
| A rebuilt architecture project | Platform/workspace/dashboard contracts are **set** |

## Target market

### Primary beachhead (recommended)

**Business administrators at organizations with 10–200 employees** who need a unified workspace with optional HR, scheduling, and custom modules.

**Why:** Business bootstrap, business billing, admin-gated marketplace, and WS-L2 certified business workspace shell.

### Secondary

**Power personal users** — freelancers, prosumer productivity users who want modular AI-aware dashboard.

### Tertiary (operator-led)

**Module developers and marketplace partners** — technical partners building on module contract.

### Deferred

**Enterprise (500+)** until status page, support SLAs, compliance artifacts, and business paid-module billing E2E are complete.

## Competitors (category framing)

| Competitor / category | Their strength | Vssyl differentiation |
|----------------------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Notion / Coda** | Flexible docs + databases | Real **module marketplace** + business workspace shell + AI context graph |
| **Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace** | Suite breadth, enterprise trust | **Modular** — install what you need; unified AI context across installed apps |
| **Slack + best-of-breed** | Best-in-class per tool | **One workspace** — shared dashboard, billing, and AI context |
| **Monday / ClickUp** | Project management | Broader workspace + **partner modules** (HR, scheduling, etc.) |
| **Salesforce Platform** | Enterprise extensibility | Lighter-weight; SMB-focused modular workspace |

**Do not compete on:** "We have chat and calendar" — table stakes.  
**Compete on:** Modular workspace + connected AI context + business module ecosystem.

## Competitive advantages (real in code today)

1. **Unified personal + business** in one user account with separate workspace scopes
2. **Marketplace with certification pipeline** — browse, install, scope enforcement (L2.5 backend)
3. **PP-3 billing** — DB-backed pricing, personal/business/module subscriptions, entitlements
4. **AI context system** — module providers, retrieval, context graph (architecture mature)
5. **WS-L2 certified reference workspace** — business and personal shells production-viable
6. **First-party module depth** — Drive, Chat, Calendar, HR, Scheduling, etc. with UX certification
7. **Developer submission path** — metadata, GCS artifact upload, admin approval
