# Enterprise Module Strategy (superseded body)

**Status:** Compact principle stub — **not** commercial packaging SoT, SKU catalog, or architecture law.

## Settled product principle

**Enhance the existing Vssyl application/module family for enterprise needs rather than creating separate enterprise forks** of those applications (e.g. no parallel “enterprise HR” product tree).

Enterprise may eventually deepen governance, permissions, integrations, support/service levels, configuration, compliance, entitlements, and commercial packaging — within **one product family**.

## Open (not settled here)

Commercial meaning of “enterprise,” per-module premium SKUs, sales model, and governance/compliance/service packaging remain **open**. See [`commercialOpenDecisions.md`](./commercialOpenDecisions.md).
