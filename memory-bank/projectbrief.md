# Project Brief

**Authority:** Durable project identity and vision for Vssyl.  
**Not:** Implementation truth, architecture law, current status, module catalog, pricing policy, or AI mechanics.

This document orients humans and agents to *what Vssyl is and why it exists*. Deeper product, architecture, and commercial detail live in dedicated owners.

---

## What Vssyl is

Vssyl is **one contextual operational platform** for people and organizations.

It helps work and life operate across connected areas—files, communication, schedules, people, places, and related domains—without forcing everything into disconnected software silos. Applications participate in one platform and can share governed context while remaining distinct domains.

Vssyl is not a chatbot bolted onto apps, not a single monolithic domain, and not a marketing feature bundle.

---

## Why it exists

People and organizations routinely run through fragmented tools: duplicated context, disconnected workflows, and software that adds administrative burden instead of reducing it.

Vssyl’s thesis is that software can carry enough **governed context** to keep work connected—while preserving clear domain ownership and user control—so people spend less time reconstructing state across systems.

---

## Who it serves

Vssyl serves **individuals and organizations** through two central governed contexts:

- **Personal** — personal work, preferences, and relationships within personal authority
- **Business** — organization and tenant work within business authority and policy

Personal and Business share platform capabilities and intelligence while retaining appropriate **authority, privacy, and context boundaries**. Cross-scope access or knowledge transfer is never assumed merely because someone uses both. Detailed boundaries live in product and AI authority.

---

## Durable principles

1. **One platform** — Applications participate in one contextual platform rather than behaving as disconnected SaaS products.
2. **Domain ownership** — Applications remain responsible for their own domain records and workflows.
3. **Shared capabilities** — Common platform capabilities connect applications without erasing domain boundaries.
4. **Context over fragmentation** — Relevant governed context should travel with the user rather than forcing repeated reconstruction.
5. **Reduce burden** — The platform should reduce cognitive and operational administration, not become another system users constantly maintain.
6. **Real-world concepts** — Prefer language and concepts people understand from life and business; push technical abstraction deeper into the platform.
7. **User control** — Context, intelligence, and automation remain governed; they must not silently override users or domain authority.
8. **One product family** — Customer scale deepens capabilities within the same application family rather than creating separate enterprise forks of each app.

---

## Contextual intelligence

Vssyl is designed to **understand context**, not merely store data or attach a chatbot to applications.

- Vssyl provides governed identity, truth, relationships, and authority around intelligence.
- Applications remain authoritative for domain truth.
- Intelligence inherits the user’s and domain’s authority; it does not expand it.
- The underlying model or provider is replaceable and is **not** Vssyl’s product identity.

Durable AI product philosophy: [`aiProductPhilosophy.md`](./aiProductPhilosophy.md).

## Ecosystem (high level)

Vssyl supports first-party and extensible application capabilities inside one governed platform. Creator/publisher surfaces, discovery/installation, and platform approval/certification are separated by responsibility. Commercial packaging and creator economics are product-policy concerns—not identity.

Commercial packaging does **not** define Vssyl’s identity. Pricing structure, enterprise commercial meaning, premium packaging, creator economics, and AI monetization remain explicit open product-policy questions.

## What Vssyl is not

- A bundle of disconnected SaaS applications
- One monolithic domain that owns everything
- A Dashboard-owned data model
- An AI layer that replaces authoritative domain systems
- Unrestricted universal context
- Separate enterprise forks of each application
